# Playbook — what we know works

The agent updates this file weekly (step 4: self-adaptation). Every rule has evidence and a sample size. Fewer than 5 posts of a variant → **low confidence**. Every change is logged at the bottom.

## Audience
- **Fermieri** (trout farmers, Romania, mostly mountain areas: Brașov, Neamț, Suceava, Argeș, Hunedoara, Maramureș). Care about: water temperature and oxygen in summer, fish health and losses, feed cost per kg of fish (FCR), selling the fish. Practical, sceptical of marketing, respect peers and numbers.
- **Consumatori** (Romanians who buy fish). Eat 2.6× less fish than the EU average. Care about: is farmed fish safe („hormoni”, „antibiotice”), how to cook trout, price, local origin.

## Voice
Expert neighbour, not a brochure. Calm, concrete, a bit warm. Explain *why*. Show respect for the farmer's experience.
