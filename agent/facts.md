## Consumption
- Romania ate **8.75 kg** of fish and seafood per person in 2023; EU average **22.89 kg** (≈ 2.6×). Source: EUMOFA, *The EU fish market 2025*, apparent consumption 2023 — https://eumofa.eu · checked 2026-11.

## Safety / regulation
- **Growth hormones are banned** in EU farm animals, including fish (Directive 96/22/EC, as amended). https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/1996/22/oj · checked 2026-11.
- **Veterinary antibiotics only on prescription**, no preventive group use, no growth promotion (Regulation (EU) 2019/6, art. 105–107). https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2019/6/oj · checked 2026-11.
- **VHS** (septicemia hemoragică virală) and **IHN** (necroza hematopoietică infecțioasă) are *listed diseases* of salmonids under the EU Animal Health Law (Regulation (EU) 2016/429; Implementing Regulation (EU) 2018/1882). Suspicion must be reported to the official veterinarian (in Romania: DSVSA / ANSVSA). https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2018/1882/oj · checked 2026-11.
- There is **no treatment for viral diseases** in fish; control is by prevention (healthy-certified stock, biosecurity, disinfection, low stress). Wording: „nu există tratament”, never „furajul previne”.

## Fish health (BioMar, Fish Health 101 series)
- Fish have two immune responses: **innate** (fast, first line: skin, mucus, gills) and **adaptive** (specific, remembers pathogens). Both need energy and nutrients. BioMar post 23.11.2026.
- Healthy **skin and gills** are important barriers against external challenges. BioMar post 22.10.2026.
- Virus-infected fish do not always show visible signs at first. BioMar post 30.10.2026.
